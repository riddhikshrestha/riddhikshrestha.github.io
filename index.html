Ejection‑fraction estimation from echocardiography

Research — deep learning

A contrastive‑learning pipeline that estimates left ventricular ejection fraction directly from echocardiography video, trained on the EchoNet‑Dynamic dataset. A ResNet‑18 encoder feeds a multi‑head self‑attention block for temporal modelling across the cardiac cycle, optimised with a combination of NT‑Xent and triplet‑margin losses.

Built the full pipeline end to end — data loading, training, evaluation — alongside a literature review and a publication‑ready paper.
